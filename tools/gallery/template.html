How the scores work

Each site was opened in a real browser, at desktop and at phone width, and measured. Every category is scored out of 100, and the overall score is the plain average of the four. Rank 1 is the best of rated sites; sites with the same score share a rank.

These are automated estimates, not a teacher's grade. They read what the page does, not how much effort or learning went into it. A calm, minimal page can score lower than a busy one, and a site can lose points for a slow start or a long intro screen. Sites that were offline or would not load are not rated. Use the ranking to compare, not to judge a student.
