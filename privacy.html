Privacy

Last updated 17 November 2026

learning-today.com is a small learner site. It has no accounts, no forms and no sign-in, so it never asks you for your name, email or any other detail.

This page says what the site does collect, which other companies see your visit, and how to ask for something to be changed or removed.

Analytics

We use Google Analytics (GA4) to count visits and see which pages are read. It records things like the page you viewed, your approximate location (country or city), your device and browser, and how you arrived. It sets cookies to do this. We do not use it to identify you.

The Google Analytics script loads only after the page has finished loading, or after you first scroll, tap or press a key. Google's own policy is at policies.google.com/privacy. If you do not want to be counted, you can install Google's opt-out browser add-on or block analytics in your browser.

Other companies that see your visit

To show a page, your browser contacts other servers. Each of them can see your IP address and your browser details:

  • Google Fonts (fonts.googleapis.com and fonts.gstatic.com) supplies the typefaces.
  • unpkg.com supplies a small script, only in older browsers that lack a built-in menu feature.
  • The student websites page shows a live preview of each student's own website. Loading the page therefore contacts those sites too (most are hosted on Lovable, Netlify, Vercel and similar). Their own privacy policies apply.
  • Links and downloads go to PIAIC, Panaversity, Anthropic, Google Docs and other sites. Their policies apply once you leave this one.

This site sets no cookies of its own other than the analytics cookies above.

Student websites and admin photos

The student websites page links to sites that students built and posted in the class group. It shows each site's own public title and description. We remove phone numbers and email addresses that appear in those descriptions, and we do not publish who posted a link.

The scores and ranks on that page are automated estimates made by measuring each page. They are not a grade or an assessment of any person.

The photos beside the admins' names on the Links shared in Class page come from their official public pages (panaversity.org and aneeqkhatri.com).

Asking for a change or removal

If you built one of the listed sites, appear in a photo here, or want anything corrected or taken down, open an issue on the site's GitHub page and say which page and which item. Please do not put private details in a public issue. We will remove or change it promptly.

Changes

We update this page when the site changes. The date at the top shows when it last did.

learning-today.com is an independent learner site. PIAIC and Anthropic run the official programme and exams; this site only points to them and records what one class has covered.
